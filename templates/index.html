Student Attendance Check-in

Enter your roll number and verify your face with the webcam. Student self check-in is available daily from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM (school server local time). Your face must be enrolled by staff with student or guardian consent. Enrollment photos and a face template are stored in the school's attendance database; the live scan is compared with the profile for your roll number. Only a successful match can mark attendance.

Camera is off.

Allow camera access when prompted. Face matching needs an internet connection to load the recognition model. If your face is not recognized, contact faculty for help.
